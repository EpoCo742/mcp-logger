The standard in one sentence

Every production AI interaction leaves one record that is complete, attributable, provable and tamper-evident.

Complete

What went in, what came out, and every value the AI used along the way.

Attributable

The firm, the person, the client and the agent involved, each one verified.

Provable

Every fact in an answer is tied to the source record it came from.

Tamper-evident

Any change to a record after the fact is detectable, and the record is kept for its full term.

This is the sentence to remember. Everything that follows is how the four words are delivered: where capture happens, what the record holds, and how it is protected.
