The existing rules already cover AI

Rule or frameworkWhat it asks of usHow the standard answers
SEC Rule 17a-4Records kept so they cannot be altered or erased, or with a complete audit trail of changesAppend-only archive, hash chain and independently timestamped checkpoints
FINRA Rule 4511Books and records made and preserved for prescribed periodsA retention class assigned to every AI interaction at the point of entry
NIST AI Risk Management FrameworkAI systems that are traceable, transparent and accountableOne correlated record per interaction, including model version and settings
ISO/IEC 42001A management system for AI with logging and impact controlsOne standard applied to every production AI use, with conformance testing
EU AI Act, Article 12Automatic event logging for AI systems in regulated usesCapture happens automatically at shared gateways, not in each application

Technical building blocks are open standards: W3C Trace Context for correlation, RFC 8785 canonical JSON for hashing, RFC 3161 timestamps, Ed25519 signatures.

None of these rules carve out AI. The point of the slide: we are not inventing a new obligation, we are making sure AI-mediated activity meets the ones we already have. Legal and Compliance should confirm the mapping before the standard is ratified.
