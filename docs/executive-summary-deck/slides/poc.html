The proof of concept runs all four enforcement points, mock systems of record and the Audit Plane as real services talking over HTTP, with a web interface that replays any interaction and verifies the archive live. It can run against a real Claude model or a deterministic stand-in. Keys, the archive and the time authority are simulated; the structure and the record format are what the standard specifies.
